Third-party material folded into this bundle
=============================================

The following files were incorporated from two separate MIT-licensed
TextMate bundles by Stefan Daschek, to add YAML front-matter and MathJax
support directly to this Markdown bundle. Shebangs were changed from
`ruby18`/`ruby20` to the system `ruby` to suit this fork's toolchain;
the logic is otherwise unmodified.

From noniq/Markdown-Front-Matter.tmbundle
(https://github.com/noniq/Markdown-Front-Matter.tmbundle):
  - Syntaxes/Markdown (Front Matter).tmLanguage
  - Preferences/Markdown Filter.tmPreferences
  - Support/strip_front_matter

From noniq/Markdown-MathJax.tmbundle
(https://github.com/noniq/Markdown-MathJax.tmbundle):
  - Preferences/TM_MARKDOWN_FILTER_POST.tmPreferences
  - Support/append_mathjax_js
